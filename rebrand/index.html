Rebrand review — local only, nothing deployed

Eight one-word names a barista already says, each with a mark drawn from its own meaning.

Why portafile isn't landing: it's a portmanteau you have to be told — portafilter + file. Said out loud people write down "porta-feel," or assume it's file storage, which is the wrong category entirely. Every name below is a word your customers already use on shift, so it needs no footnote. Availability is .com only, checked directly against the Verisign registry. No domain has been bought and none will be.

The names

The palettes

Kraft

What you have now. Kraft paper, espresso ink, crema gold, terracotta. Built for a paper-first product — laminated cards in a daylight café. Deliberately not SaaS blue. Nothing is wrong with it; the logo is the weak part, not the color.

Counter

The machine, not the card. Dark roasted ground, warm amber, steel. Looks expensive and modern on screen. Cost: your product's whole point is printing cards — this palette fights the paper, and dark UI prints badly.

Daylight

Third-wave clean. Near-white, true black, deep green, one hot orange. Reads more current and more premium than Kraft, still prints clean. The closest thing to a real upgrade rather than a sideways move.

How to use this page: pick one name and one palette. I'll then draw the finished logo and wordmark properly, and roll the name through all 53 files that say "Portafile" — landing pages, the Portuguese site, the app, the guides, the test markers.

What will not change, ever: the storage keys portafile.v1.* and the x-portafile-* headers. Those are identifiers, not branding — renaming them would erase every real café's saved shop and break devices already in the field.

Out of scope: the launch film says the old name aloud and can't be cheaply re-narrated.
