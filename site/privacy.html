Privacy Policy

Effective July 22, 2026

The short version

Your shop's data — recipes, prices, dial-in settings, sign-offs — lives in your own browser by default and never leaves your device. If you choose to turn on Sync, a copy is also stored in Portafile's cloud storage so your staff's devices can share it — encrypted in transit, private (not readable from the open internet), and reachable only through your shop's secret link. There is no account system, and the only personal information we ever collect is your email address, if you choose to give it.

Cost data and your staff

What you pay for milk, beans and cups is between you and your suppliers. Devices that join through your staff link get recipes, builds, dial-ins and training — with ingredient costs, margins, the price table and the margin report hidden. Only devices holding your owner code see cost data, and our server — not the device — decides which is which. Printed recipe cards, station sheets and new-hire packs never include costs; the margin report is owner-only. Resetting the staff link (Data → Reset staff link) cuts off every old device and erases the shop data stored on them.

What we collect

Nothing, by default. Using the Portafile app requires no account, no email, and no personal information. All app data is stored locally in your browser and is under your control; you can export or delete it at any time (Data → Export, or clear your browser data).

Your shop's data, only if you turn on Sync. Sync is off by default. When you turn it on, your shop's data is stored in a private cloud store (Vercel Blob) so devices with your link share it. Turn off sync, and delete or overwrite the data, any time from the Data menu; write to us and we'll delete your shop's cloud copy entirely.

Your email address, if you give it. If you sign up for updates or purchase a plan, we collect your email to send onboarding tips, product updates, and receipts. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link; unsubscribing stops marketing email immediately and permanently.

Payment information is handled entirely by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Server logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard, short-lived access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and operations, as all web hosts do.

What we don't do

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, no cookie banners needed — the site sets no cookies.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone.
  • We never read your individual recipes or costs, and we never share or sell them. Without sync they are not on our servers at all; with sync they sit in private storage that exists only to serve your shop's devices.
  • We do count, across all shops together, how many are using the app and which parts of it they use — for example "60% of shops use training sign-offs". Those are totals only. No recipe, price, or shop name is ever shown or exported, not even to us.
  • If you turn on Sync, we record the calendar dates your shop used the app, and when your shop was set up and last changed — so we can tell whether it's actually helping you and call you if it isn't. Dates only: never times, never how long, never which person or device. Nothing about what you did that day is recorded, and this list is deleted with the rest of your data.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with one click, or write to us and we'll delete your email address from our records entirely. Your app data needs no request — delete it yourself anytime from your browser.

Children

Portafile is a business tool for coffee shops and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be announced by email to subscribers.
