CHAPTER 01

The best shops run on recipes nobody wrote down.

The build for the house latte lives in one person's hands. The grind setting from Tuesday lives in their memory. When that person takes a Saturday off, the drinks change, and the only person who notices is the regular who stops coming.

Everybody knows this. Nobody has ten spare hours to write a binder that goes stale in a month.

CHAPTER 04

One tool, three jobs your binder cannot do.

The money

Type what you pay for milk, beans and cups once. Every card shows drink-level cost, margin and COGS%, and re-prices itself the moment an ingredient moves. When your roaster raises prices you know which drinks went underwater before the till does.

Printed cards never show a cost. The margin report is the owner-only page, and staff devices genuinely never receive the numbers.

The morning

Grind, dose, yield and time, logged per bean in five seconds. The board shows the current setting, every card carries it, and the history is still there when that Ethiopia comes back next season.

The person opening on Sunday gets Tuesday's answer without texting anyone.

The Monday

Order your training path, hit print: cover page, day-by-day checklist, every recipe card, today's dial-ins. Sign-offs tracked per barista. The knowledge stops walking out the door with whoever wrote the old binder.

Nobody unlocks a phone with wet hands at 7:58am. Crema prints laminate-ready cards and a station sheet, while the app stays the living source of truth.

CHAPTER 05

Asked over the counter.

The six questions owners actually open with.

Where does my data live?

In your browser, on your machine. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on Sync. Sync keeps a private cloud copy so staff phones share the same live data through a secret link. Export a JSON backup from the Data menu and keep it with your shop docs. Details in the privacy policy.

Can my staff see what my drinks cost me?

No. Devices on the staff link get recipes, builds, dial-ins and training. Ingredient costs never reach them: the server keeps the real numbers in a separate place and only hands them to a device that proves it is yours. Printed cards and new-hire packs never show costs either.

Why is it free? What is the catch?

No catch. It is free the way a sample bag is free. If Crema earns a place taped to your machine, a synced paid tier is where I would eventually make a living. If it does not, you lost nothing and you keep your export.

Could I not just use Google Docs?

You could, and shops have been able to for fifteen years. Docs will not compute your margin at today's milk price, will not remember your grind setting, and will not print a 4×6 card. The blank page is the enemy. Crema starts you with fifteen editable drinks.

Does it work without wifi?

Yes. It runs entirely in the browser and loads no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else. A dead router does not close your recipe cards.

Do my baristas need accounts?

No accounts anywhere, for anyone. Baristas use the printed packs or the shop device. Sign-offs are tracked by name, not by login.
