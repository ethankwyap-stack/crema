portafile
Your whole shop, dialed in.

The operating system for independent coffee shops, built on the recipe card.

Independent cafes run on knowledge nobody wrote down: recipes in one person's head, grind settings shouted across the bar, training that consists of "shadow Maya, good luck." Portafile turns that into living cards that know their cost, their margin, their current dial-in, and how to train Monday's new hire — then prints onto the paper that actually survives a morning rush.

The problem, in their words

"[C]offee like no other, has the absolute WORST training standards… 9/10 of the coffee shops I visit these days have absolutely no standards."cafe owner, 7 yrs specialty — r/barista, Apr 2025 (fetched, cited in repo)
"[W]e really don't have a ideal recipe book to make sure things and drinks are being made the same…"barista, nonprofit cafe — r/barista, Apr 2024

Why now

  • Input costs broke the laminated price list: arabica peaked at $4.41/lb (2025, a historic high); 50% Brazil tariff cut US imports 46% in one month. Drink costing became a recurring problem. (Perfect Daily Grind, Feb 2026)
  • Turnover made training a treadmill: food-service quit rate 4.3%/mo — ~2× private sector (Coffee Intelligence, Jun 2026); 75% of indie owners call staffing their #1 challenge (2025 Indie Coffee Report).
  • The tooling gap is real: coffee ops suites run $50–900/mo, built for multi-location; Trainual won't show a price without a demo call. The single-location shop's current tool is a binder.

Moat (what AI templates can't copy)

  • Living state: this shop's dial-in history, ingredient costs, sign-off records — generated docs rot the day the bean changes.
  • Paper-first output where incumbents ship dashboards.
  • Roaster network: every partner roaster seeds Portafile into every new wholesale account.

Status (all verifiable in repo)

  • Product v1 built & verified: 12/12 e2e checks, zero errors; runs offline, $0 infra.
  • Brand + site + 75s launch film shipped.
  • Adversarially reviewed (3-skeptic panel pre-build, 4-killer panel post-build; verdicts in repo).
  • Domains checked available: portafile.io, getportafile.com.
The honest part. Beachhead ceiling at 3% penetration ≈ ~$200k ARR — this is a wedge, not the endgame. Expansion axes: Group tier (2–5 locations), roaster network, and a deliberately vertical-agnostic core (bars, bakeries, juice — same undocumented-knowledge disease, >5× the venues). Validation gates (15 owner-content submissions / 10 founding prepays in 30 days) are defined in the plan with kill criteria. No revenue yet; no customers yet; this teaser is the plan of record, not a trading history.
